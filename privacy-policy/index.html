Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy for Southeast Adventures LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you:

  • Download or use any of our mobile applications, including Michi, Marizz, MemRays, NaniNavi, FamFind or any other app that links to this Privacy Policy

Questions or concerns? If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. For questions or concerns, contact us at southeastadventures5@gmail.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal Information You Disclose to Us

In Short: We collect personal information you choose to provide.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address (used for service messages, updates, or optional marketing)

Application Data

Depending on the app and features used, we may request:

  • Access to contacts
  • Access to reminders or notifications
  • Access to photos
  • Permission to send push notifications

These permissions are optional and can be managed in your device settings.

Information Automatically Collected

We automatically collect:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Log and usage data
  • Location data (precise or approximate, depending on device settings)

This information helps maintain security, analytics, and app functionality. We do not intentionally process sensitive personal information.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We may process your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Deliver app services
  • Provide support
  • Manage payments and subscriptions
  • Send product updates or promotional communications (optional)
  • Protect individuals’ safety and vital interests

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

We only process information when we have a valid legal basis, including:

  • Your consent
  • Contract performance
  • Legal obligations
  • Legitimate interests
  • Vital interests

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Personal Information?

We may share information:

  • During a business transfer (e.g., merger or acquisition)
  • With service providers
  • If displaying a third-party offer wall (if applicable)

We do not sell personal data.

5. What Is Our Stance on Third-Party Websites?

We are not responsible for any third-party websites, services, or applications linked within our apps. Their data practices are governed by their own policies.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. When no longer needed, information is deleted, anonymized, or isolated until deletion is possible.

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards. However, no system is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee protection from unauthorized access or misuse.

8. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

We do not knowingly collect or market data from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that such data was collected, we will delete it promptly. To report concerns, email southeastadventures5@gmail.com.

9. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada, or certain U.S. states), you may have rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent

You may exercise rights by contacting southeastadventures5@gmail.com. Marketing emails can be opted out of at any time via unsubscribe links.

12. Do We Make Updates to This Policy?
