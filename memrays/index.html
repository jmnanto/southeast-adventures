memrays: A Faith and Gratitude Journal

An answered prayer or a moment of gratitude can be easy to forget by the end of the week. memrays gives those experiences a place of their own, with photos and tags that help you find a particular entry when you want to revisit it.

Available as a free download for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 14 or later.

More from the app

How memrays works

  1. Write down what happened and why it mattered to you.
  2. Add a photo if it helps you remember, and tag the entry with a topic such as prayer or gratitude.
  3. Filter by a tag to revisit related entries. Share an experience with someone if you choose.

When to use memrays

Keep a record of answered prayers, collect insights from scripture study, or look back at small acts of service you’ve noticed throughout the year.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find all my entries about a specific topic?
Yes. Add a tag such as prayer, family, or service to relevant entries, then filter the journal by that tag.
Can I edit a journal entry later?
Yes. You can come back to an existing entry and update it.
