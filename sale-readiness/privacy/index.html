Sale Readiness: Promo Audit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Sale Readiness: Promo Audit is operated by Southeast Adventures LLC. The app helps Shopify merchants review promotional pricing, potential discount conflicts, product availability, inventory, and collection readiness before a sale.

Sale Readiness is a read-only inspection tool. It does not modify your Shopify products, prices, or discounts.

Information We Access

With your authorization, Sale Readiness accesses Shopify store information using the read_products and read_discounts permissions.

This information may include:

  • Product names, identifiers, variants, SKUs, images, and pricing.
  • Product availability, inventory quantities, and selling policies.
  • Product status, publication information, and collection membership.
  • Discount codes, configurations, eligibility restrictions, dates, and applicable products or collections.
  • Store currency and time zone information.

Sale Readiness does not request access to customer or order records for its inspections. However, product and catalog information may contain personal information entered by the merchant.

How We Use Information

Sale Readiness retrieves and analyzes store information to identify potential issues before a promotion.

Inspection information is processed by our application server and returned to your authenticated browser. The app does not maintain a permanent database of product inspection results. To help you return to a recent inspection after Shopify purchase approval or navigation, it may save one encrypted inspection checkpoint per store in your browser’s session storage. The checkpoint includes inspection results and settings, is encrypted and authenticated by the server, and is valid for no more than 30 minutes after completion. Restoring an inspection or purchasing access does not extend that expiry. Restoration requires Shopify authentication, validation for the same store, and a current access check before detailed paid findings are returned.

This checkpoint is a temporary convenience, not a permanent report archive or a live view of your catalog. Browser restrictions, storage limits, a different tab or browser session, or cleared site data may prevent saving or restoration. When a checkpoint expires or cannot be restored, you can run a new inspection. The server rejects expired checkpoints, but cannot guarantee physical deletion of browser-held copies at exactly 30 minutes. You can remove locally saved checkpoints by clearing the app’s site data; server-side store deletion does not remotely erase browser storage.

Inspection settings, selected identifiers, and optional promotion dates may appear in application requests or URLs. Information saved in your browser history or copied to your device remains subject to your browser and device settings.

Information We Store

To operate the service, Sale Readiness stores certain information associated with your Shopify store.

Authentication information: Store domain, session identifiers, authorization details, access credentials, and expiration information. Shopify may also provide merchant or staff identifiers, names, email addresses, locale, and account-related information used for authentication.

Purchase and access information: Shopify purchase identifiers, test-purchase indicators, activation dates, access-expiration dates, and pending purchase information. These records support purchase verification, 30-day Full Inspection access, and recovery from interrupted purchase approvals.

Shopify processes payments and billing approvals. Sale Readiness does not collect or store payment-card details.

Support communications: If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

Service Providers and Security

Sale Readiness uses third-party services to operate:

  • Shopify: Store authentication, data access, and billing.
  • Google Cloud: Application hosting and operational logging.
  • Neon: Securely hosted PostgreSQL application database.

Our public information website uses GitHub Pages, and support communications are handled through Gmail.

These services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request URLs, timestamps, and diagnostic events.

We use service-provider security features and application controls to help protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Sale Readiness does not include advertising trackers or a separate merchant analytics integration. Shopify and our infrastructure providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their services. Sale Readiness does not add its own advertising or tracking cookies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Sale Readiness's 30-day Full Inspection access period determines how long purchased functionality remains available. It does not determine how long application records are retained.

Authentication, purchase, and pending-purchase records do not have a fixed automatic deletion schedule. Certain pending purchase records may be removed when their status is resolved.

When you uninstall Sale Readiness, Shopify access ends. The app attempts to remove associated session records when the necessary session information is available. Purchase records and some other application records may remain after uninstall.

When Sale Readiness successfully processes a verified Shopify store-deletion privacy request, it removes the associated store's session, purchase, and pending-purchase records from its active application database.

Sale Readiness also processes Shopify customer privacy notifications when received. The app does not maintain a customer or order database.

Deletion from the active database does not necessarily remove information from backups, recovery systems, operational logs, support correspondence, Shopify's own records, or copies saved by merchants.

Operational logs are retained according to our hosting configuration. Database backups and recovery copies are subject to separate retention processes. Support communications may be retained as needed to address inquiries and applicable obligations.

Contact us if you have questions about retained information or wish to request deletion.
