Sale Readiness: Promo Audit helps Shopify merchants review pricing, discounts, availability, and collections before a promotion goes live.

Four areas. One clearer starting point.

Review the details that matter.

  • Sale pricingReview unusual promotional pricing patterns and confirm that markdowns are intentional.
  • Potential discount conflictsIdentify discounts that may apply to products already on sale. Actual eligibility depends on the discount and checkout conditions.
  • Product availability and inventoryReview publication and inventory observations that may affect products intended for your promotion.
  • Collection readinessReview collection observations, including membership, active products, and publication evidence where it can be verified.

Prepare → Inspect → Review

Turn findings into informed decisions.

  1. Prepare your inspection. Choose the entire catalog, a collection, or selected products, with optional promotion dates.
  2. Run a free inspection. See summary counts and statuses across all four readiness categories.
  3. Unlock the details when you need them. Review affected products, discounts, collections, and suggested manual checks with Full Inspection.
  4. Review records directly in Shopify Admin. Product, discount, and collection links open in a new tab so you can keep your findings open.

Sale Readiness is read-only. It does not edit products, change prices, create discounts, or automatically fix issues. Findings help guide your review; they do not guarantee that every issue will be detected. Coverage limits and unavailable evidence are disclosed in the app. Illustrative discount prices are not guaranteed checkout outcomes, and the inspection does not assume discounts combine.
