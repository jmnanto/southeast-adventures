Sale Readiness support

Launch your next sale with confidence.

Sale Readiness: Promo Audit helps you review promotional pricing, potential discount conflicts, availability, inventory, and collections. It is read-only: you review and make any changes directly in Shopify.

Open Sale Readiness

For an existing installation, sign in to Shopify Admin, select the correct store, and open Apps → Sale Readiness. Open the app through Shopify rather than bookmarking an authentication or billing-return URL. For installation questions, contact support below.

Run a free inspection

  1. Choose your inspection scope: entire catalog, a specific collection, or selected products.
  2. Add optional promotion dates, then select Run inspection.
  3. Review the summary counts and statuses for Sale Pricing, Discount Conflicts, Product Availability and Inventory, and Collection Readiness.

Check any incomplete-coverage notices. Findings are review signals, not a guarantee that every issue has been found. Product prices reflect the inspection time; future prices are not predicted.

Select Get Sale Ready in the app, review the access terms, and continue to Shopify to approve the purchase. The app verifies approval before granting access. Later access checks do not restart the 30-day period.

Review findings in Shopify

With Full Inspection, expand the relevant results list to review affected products, discounts, or collections and suggested manual checks. Follow record links to open Shopify Admin in a new tab, keeping your findings available. Review each discount's conditions separately; an illustrative price is not a verified checkout result.

If purchase approval is interrupted

Reopen Sale Readiness through Shopify Admin for the same store and use Check purchase status before trying another purchase. If the app offers Continue to Shopify approval, use it to finish the pending approval. Do not approve a second purchase merely because access has not appeared. Contact support if verification remains unavailable or access stays locked after approval.

If the app will not open

Confirm you are signed in to the intended store and have permission to use the app, then reopen it through Shopify Admin. If the problem persists, contact support with the visible error text and approximate time. Do not send authentication or billing-return URLs.
