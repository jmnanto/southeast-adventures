Guided: Bubble Level for iPhone

When a shelf looks crooked or an appliance rocks, Guided helps you see how far out of level it is. Use your iPhone as a spirit level for straight edges, a two-axis level for flat surfaces, or an inclinometer when you need an angle reading.

For iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Free to download with a one-time Guided Pro upgrade.

How Guided works

  1. Choose Classic for an edge, Surface to check tilt in two directions, or Angle to read inclination.
  2. Rest the phone against the edge or flat on the surface and read the display. Use calibration if you need to establish a reference.
  3. Use the available visual, vibration, or audio feedback as you make adjustments.

When to use Guided

Check a picture frame or shelf in Classic mode, set up a washing machine with Surface mode, or use Angle mode to measure the tilt of a work surface.

Frequently asked questions

Can I check a tabletop in both directions?
Yes. Lay the iPhone flat and switch to Surface mode to see tilt in two axes.
Can I level toward an angle other than zero?
Guided Pro adds custom target angles, directional visual guidance, and extra themes. It is a one-time purchase.
Do I need to keep watching the screen?
Guided offers audio and vibration feedback so you can pay attention to what you’re adjusting.
