marizz: A Relationship Quiz for Dating

It can be hard to put into words what’s going well with the person you’re dating and what still gives you pause. marizz walks you through questions about relationship traits so you can see how your own impressions add up.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 14 or later. Try one quiz free. Continued use requires a lifetime Premium purchase.

How marizz works

  1. Think of the person you’re dating and work through the quiz prompts.
  2. Answer each question based on what you’ve observed.
  3. Review the score and think about which answers you’d like to explore further.

When to use marizz

Use it on your own after a few dates or with friends as a starting point for a conversation about what you each value in a relationship.

Frequently asked questions

What does the quiz score mean?
The score summarizes your responses. You can use it to reflect on your impressions or identify topics you want to discuss.
Does the other person need to take the quiz?
You can complete it yourself using your own observations.
