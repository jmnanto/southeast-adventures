michi: Compare Options With a Weighted Decision Matrix

A pros-and-cons list can hide the fact that one factor matters much more than the others. michi helps you compare choices by weighting your priorities and rating each option against the same criteria. The visual breakdown makes it easier to see why one option scored higher.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 14 or later. Analyze one decision free. Continued use requires a lifetime Michi Pro purchase.

How michi works

  1. Name the decision and add the options you’re considering.
  2. Choose criteria and give more weight to the factors that matter most to you.
  3. Rate each option against those criteria, then review the visual breakdown and compare the trade-offs.

When to use michi

Compare job offers by schedule and commute, apartments by rent and location, or laptops by battery life, price, and features.

Frequently asked questions

Can one factor count more than another?
Yes. Give the more important criterion a higher weight so it contributes more to the comparison.
Can I save my comparisons?
Michi Pro lets you save and revisit past decisions and create additional projects.
