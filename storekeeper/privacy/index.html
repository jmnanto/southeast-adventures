Privacy policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026
Storekeeper is operated by Southeast Adventures LLC.

Storekeeper is a read-only Shopify catalog inspection tool. It helps merchants find catalog issues and prepare cleanup worklists. Corrections are made directly in Shopify.

Information the app accesses

With your authorization, Storekeeper reads active product and variant information and collection information through Shopify. This includes identifiers, titles, product status, vendors, collection membership, SKUs, barcodes, SEO titles and descriptions, and image identifiers and alternative text. It uses these fields to identify catalog issues and prepare inspection results and exports. It does not edit your catalog.

The current app does not request access to customer or order records and does not maintain a customer or order database. Catalog fields can nevertheless contain personal information if you place it there.

Information stored by Storekeeper

  • Authentication: your Shopify shop domain, session identifiers and authorization state, access scopes, access tokens, refresh tokens where supplied, and expiration information. Session records can also contain Shopify-provided merchant or staff account details, such as name, email address, user identifier, locale, and account role flags.
  • Purchases and access: Shopify purchase identifiers, status, test indicator, purchase and access dates, and pending purchase records, including approval links and callback verification state. These records support purchase verification, access control, and recovery from interrupted approval.
  • Complimentary access: code hashes and administrative code settings, store-specific redemption records and access dates, and code-attempt counts and window timestamps. The code administration system stores a hash rather than the redeemable code itself.
  • Technical information: hosting and application systems process request and diagnostic metadata, which can include IP addresses, browser information, request information, timestamps, response status, and errors, to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Shopify handles purchase approval and billing. Storekeeper does not collect payment-card details.

Inspection results saved in your browser

Catalog information is processed on the application server to produce results. The application database does not contain a catalog or inspection-results table. A completed inspection is returned to your browser as a signed snapshot containing results and affected-record information.

Storekeeper attempts to save your most recent completed inspection for the verified store in your browser’s IndexedDB storage. It also uses session storage as a lookup hint. A newer successfully saved inspection replaces the previous saved inspection for that store. Saved results can be restored after refresh, navigation, or a billing return in the same browser context.

Snapshots are valid for 30 days from inspection completion. Refreshing or purchasing access does not renew that validity. Restoration verifies the signature, store identity, and expiration on the server; a saved snapshot does not itself grant Full Inspection access. The app removes expired or invalid local checkpoints when checking storage, but this is not a guarantee that all copies are physically deleted at exactly 30 days.

A signature protects integrity; it is not encryption of browser-saved content. People with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access its saved data. Browser restrictions, private browsing, cleared site data, or storage limits can prevent saving or restoration. If saving fails, results can remain usable in the current session. Browser-saved results are not a cloud backup or a live view of your catalog.

You can remove locally saved results by clearing Storekeeper’s site data in your browser. Uninstalling the app or requesting server-side deletion does not remotely erase browser storage or downloaded files.

Exports

When you request an export, your browser sends the signed inspection snapshot to Storekeeper. The server verifies the snapshot and your current access before generating a CSV, Excel, or PDF download. Export files you save or share are under your control; server-side deletion does not remove those copies.

Service providers

Storekeeper uses Shopify for authentication, catalog API access, and billing; Google Cloud for application hosting and operational logging; and Neon for its operational PostgreSQL database. These providers process information needed to deliver those functions. These public information pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. Provider processing and backups are separate from your browser’s saved results.

If you email us, we process your email address and the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. Email correspondence is handled through Gmail and retained as needed to handle requests and meet applicable obligations, without a fixed retention period. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or catalog data.

Retention, uninstall, and deletion

The 30-day Full Inspection access period and 30-day snapshot validity are not general retention periods for server records. The application has no fixed automatic age-based deletion schedule for authentication, purchase, pending-purchase, redemption, or attempt records.

Uninstalling revokes the app’s Shopify access, but Storekeeper does not immediately delete its database records on the uninstall notification. When it receives and verifies Shopify’s shop-deletion compliance webhook, it deletes the matching store’s session, purchase, pending-purchase, complimentary-redemption, and code-attempt records. This does not change Shopify’s own billing history. General code-management records are separate from those store-specific records.

Storekeeper verifies Shopify’s customer-data request and customer-deletion compliance notifications. Because it does not maintain customer or order records, these notifications do not cause it to collect customer records or treat merchant/staff sessions as customer records.

Backups, recovery copies, hosting logs, support correspondence, browser storage, and downloaded exports are outside that database deletion handler. Deleting live application records does not automatically erase these separate copies. In the current Google Cloud project configuration, the default log bucket has 30-day retention, while the required audit-log bucket has 400-day retention. These are log-bucket settings, not a retention period for the database, browser results, or all copies of information. The Neon database history window was previously observed as 6 hours and is subject to the current project configuration. This history window is a recovery setting, not a schedule for deleting live application records. Neon recovery history and separately retained recovery branches are distinct from live records. Recovery copies can remain after live records are deleted, and a recovery branch without an expiration does not automatically expire after 30 days. Contact us about deletion of information in these separate systems or correspondence.

Privacy requests and contact

To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your store, contact southeastadventures5@gmail.com. Include your store’s myshopify.com domain and the nature of your request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card details, or customer exports. We may need to verify that you are authorized to act for the store before responding.

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of your personal information. Contact us using the address above with requests or privacy concerns.
