Inspect. Review. Correct. Repeat.

A clear starting point for cleanup.

  1. Inspect your Shopify catalog. Run the read-only catalog checks.
  2. See which issues need attention. Start with the free summary and limited previews.
  3. Review affected products. Use Full Inspection for detailed worklists and downloadable cleanup exports.
  4. Make corrections directly in Shopify. Follow product links, make your changes, and reinspect.

Findings guide your review. They do not guarantee that every catalog problem has been found. Saved inspections describe your catalog at the displayed inspection time.

When to run an inspection

Run a catalog audit after a CSV import, when taking over a store, or before a merchandising refresh. Review duplicate SKUs with your operations team or use the SEO checks to find products missing titles or descriptions.

Frequently asked questions

Does an inspection change product data?
Storekeeper reads the catalog and reports its findings. You make any corrections directly in Shopify Admin.
Which product SEO fields does the audit check?
It flags missing or duplicate SEO titles and meta descriptions and reports images that lack alt text.
What can I export with Full Inspection?
Full Inspection includes downloadable CSV, Excel (XLSX), and PDF files for reviewing findings and coordinating cleanup.
