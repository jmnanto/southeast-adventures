Clarity before cleanup

Know what needs attention.

  • 13 catalog checksSpot missing information, potential duplicates, SEO issues, and catalog inconsistencies.

Inspect. Review. Correct. Repeat.

A clear starting point for cleanup.

  1. Inspect your Shopify catalog. Run the read-only catalog checks.
  2. See which issues need attention. Start with the free summary and limited previews.
  3. Review affected products. Use Full Inspection for detailed worklists and downloadable cleanup exports.
  4. Make corrections directly in Shopify. Follow product links, make your changes, and reinspect.

Findings guide your review; they do not guarantee that every catalog problem has been found. Saved inspections describe your catalog at the displayed inspection time.
