Storekeeper support

Catch catalog problems early before they become bigger problems for your store.

Storekeeper inspects your Shopify catalog for issues such as missing SKUs, duplicate barcodes, missing SEO information, empty collections, and image alternative text. It is a read-only inspection tool. It does not change products or collections for you.

Open Storekeeper

For an existing installation, sign in to Shopify Admin, select the correct store, and open Apps → Storekeeper. Open the app through Shopify rather than bookmarking an authentication or billing-return URL.

Storekeeper opens inside Shopify Admin after installation. For installation questions, contact support below.

Inspect, review, correct, repeat

  1. Run an inspection of your Shopify catalog.
  2. Review the summary to see which issues need attention.
  3. With Full Inspection, review complete affected-record worklists and download cleanup exports.
  4. Follow product links to make corrections directly in Shopify, then run another inspection to check the current catalog.

Potential duplicates and inconsistencies need your review. Inspection findings are guidance, not automatic corrections or a guarantee that every catalog problem has been found.

Saved inspections and refreshes

Storekeeper tries to save your latest completed inspection in the current browser. Check the inspection date: restored results describe the catalog at that time, not its current state. Saved inspections remain valid for up to 30 days; after that, run a new inspection. Full Inspection access is checked separately.

If results do not return after refresh, reopen the app in the same browser profile and store. Browser storage restrictions, private browsing, or cleared site data can remove saved results or prevent saving. Watch for a storage warning. You can still use a completed inspection during the current session when saving is unavailable.

For a temporary restoration error, retry opening Storekeeper through Shopify Admin once your connection is stable. If the saved inspection is expired or unavailable, run a new inspection. Purchasing access does not recreate a missing inspection.

Common questions

The app will not open or shows an authorization error

Confirm you are signed in to the intended store and have permission to use the app. Reopen it through Shopify Admin. If the error continues, contact support with the approximate time, browser, and visible error text. Do not copy authentication URLs or tokens.

Full Inspection is locked after approval

Return to Storekeeper through Shopify Admin for the same store and allow access verification to complete. Do not approve another purchase merely because access has not appeared. Contact support if the issue persists.

The Unlock button is unavailable

Purchasing may not currently be available. Contact support for help; do not send payment details or arrange an off-platform payment through this page.

An export will not download

Confirm Full Inspection is active and the inspection is still valid. Retry the export and check your browser’s download controls. If access has expired, saved results do not bypass the access requirement. Downloaded files remain on your device until you remove them.

Can Storekeeper fix my products?

No. Use its worklists and Shopify product links to make corrections yourself in Shopify, then reinspect.
