Support

If you have any questions, encounter issues, or need assistance with the app, our support team is ready to help!

For any inquiries or technical support, simply reach out to us at southeastadventures5@gmail.com. We aim to respond as quickly as possible, and we’ll do our best to resolve your issue.

Common Questions

How do I get started with the app?
Download the app in the App Store and follow the onboarding prompts.
Is there help in the app?
Yes, in the lower right corner there is a Tips page that walks through the process. Also, on each page look for the “?” button that will show tips.
How do I troubleshoot problems with the app?
If you are having trouble with the app, try closing the app and restarting. If that doesn’t resolve the problem, you can reach out to us at the email below.

If you don’t find the answer you’re looking for, feel free to contact us directly by emailing southeastadventures5@gmail.com. We’re always here to assist you!

Thank you for downloading our apps! We hope they make a difference in your life!
