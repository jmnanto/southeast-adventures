Privacy

Southeast Adventures designs its apps with privacy in mind. This page describes the principles that guide how we handle information.

Information our apps use

An app may request access to information such as location only when it is needed for a feature you choose to use. The app will explain the request and rely on your device permissions.

Your choices

You can review or change app permissions at any time in your device settings. You may also contact us to ask a privacy question or request support.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@southeastadventures.com.
