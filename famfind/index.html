FamFind: A Hidden Object Game With Your Family Photos

A familiar face changes a scavenger hunt. With FamFind, you choose family photos and look for the people in them inside illustrated scenes. A child can hunt for Grandpa at a county fair, then pass the iPad to someone else for the next adventure.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later. The County Fair is free, with optional one-time adventure collection purchases.

Inside the app

How FamFind works

  1. Choose the family photos you want to play with.
  2. Pick an illustrated world, such as the free County Fair.
  3. Look around the scene for each familiar face. Choose different photos to make a new hunt.

When to use FamFind

Play together on a rainy afternoon, hand the iPad around at a family gathering, or set up a short seek-and-find game for a visit with grandparents.

Frequently asked questions

Can we play without buying a collection?
Yes. Start with the County Fair. Other scenes are available as one-time collection purchases.
What kinds of scenes are available?
The Adventure Collection includes Pirate Harbor, Valley of Wonders, and Sweet Dreams. The Animal Collection includes farm, safari, jungle, and ocean scenes.
